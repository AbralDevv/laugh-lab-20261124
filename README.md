One short funny line — the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock — entered in the
[make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae).
`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line, 115 characters, under the 200-character limit, with no trailing newline.

> "The sock looked like a dust bunny with ambition. I did what any vacuum would do: I believed in it, then inhaled it."
